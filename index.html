MY INSIGHT REFLECTION

REFLECTION

In the week that I learned about the McCumber Cube, CIA Triad, and the AAA framework, I realized that protecting information goes far beyond just setting a strong password. The CIA Triad reminds us that information must remain confidential, maintain integrity, and remain available to authorized users.

The McCumber Cube expanded my understanding by showing that security must be viewed in terms of information states, security goals, and countermeasures. It reminded me that technology, people, and policies all work together in a secure environment.

As a third-year IT student, I believe understanding these principles is the difference between simply building a system and building one that is dependable and resilient. Security by Design stood out to me because protection should be integrated from the start, not added later.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality means keeping information hidden from people who are not allowed to see it. This helps protect private data and prevents unauthorized access to sensitive information.
